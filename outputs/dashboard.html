Governance built in

The engine is built to answer the questions a board, auditor or regulator will ask.

QuestionHow the lab answers it
Who decides?Only deterministic hard-control breaches block automatically. AI anomalies are HELD for a human maker-checker.
Can we explain a decision?Every BLOCK and HOLD carries the named rules that fired, each mapped to a CERT-In recommended action.
What if the AI is wrong?Hard controls stand on their own; the AI layer only adds early warning. A false positive delays a payout for review; it never rejects one.
Will it still work next quarter?Per-client behavioural baselines are retrained on recent clean traffic; thresholds are reviewed when the HOLD rate drifts.
Can logs be trusted?Hash-chained audit trail; any deletion or edit breaks the chain.
